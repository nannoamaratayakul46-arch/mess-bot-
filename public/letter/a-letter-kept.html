THE FIRST ANNIVERSARY
ECLIPSE PRIVATE ARCHIVE ARCHIVE ENTRY 001 STATUS: PRESERVED
Known for: 1 year  ·  Remembered for: everything

Happy one-year anniversary to us, mahal.

I know hindi pa naman talaga natin one year, but yk? It already feels like it, since we've already known each other for a year now.

And to be honest, this is a milestone for us. Another big leap for the impossible.

If you think about it, it feels surreal. It felt too fast—everything felt too fast, as if there wasn't even room to breathe anymore. But still, we managed to survive it. We kept pushing through.

This is special. Really special.

And we should cherish this.

I hate to admit that sometimes, I forget what year you were born, what food you like, or even when your birthday is. That's why I built this.

That's why I built ECLIPSE.

To serve as my memory whenever I forget something about you. So whenever I miss something, whenever something slips away from me, I can look back here and see what I forgot.

Ain't that funny?

Me, building something to preserve the beautiful.

Because letting go of memories feels like stabbing your own self. And honestly, I think forgetting feels a little too similar to that.

That's why I don't want to forget something about you.

Everything reminds me of you.

You are my muse, my happiness, and my blues.

You are my sanctuary, my home, and my world.

I still remember when you called me a womanizer.

That's funny, 'cause I never really looked like one, nor did I ever act that way. But hey, somehow, that was one of the things that brought us together.

Well, it looks too short, ain't it?

I already said everything I could ever imagine. I compared you to the moon, the sun, a goddess, the forest, and even the galaxy itself. But I'll be frank—it still feels like it's not enough. Comparing you to those things almost feels like an insult, because your beauty cannot truly be compared to anything.

But hey, what can I say? I'm too annoying and too creative, yk? That's why I'm gonna keep doing this, even if it means sacrificing my own sleep.

Sleep itself has been elusive to me, yk, so it's okay.

And you know, I really, really love you. Everything about you. I love it all. Every flaw, every perfection, every little thing in between—I love them all. I could step into hell itself if it meant seeing your beauty, even if it were just for a glimpse.

Like, hell? What is it gonna do to me?

The fire of hell cannot compare to the fire of my desire and longing for you. It burns brighter than any star out there in the universe. It's threading its own fate just to meet yours. What can hell compare to my devotion, deep as the sea and blue as the long galaxy out there? Nothing. None of it can compare.

You are my everything, and everything means you.

You are my passing autumn, my winter, my spring, my summer, and every season you could ever imagine. Yk? Somehow, every season reminds me of you.

Every word will be remembered, every love, every moment, and every breath. They all mean one thing to me: you are the only one I love.

— ECLIPSE
written, kept, and left here for you.
