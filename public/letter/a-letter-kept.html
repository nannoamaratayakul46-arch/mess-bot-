THE FIRST ANNIVERSARY · 26 · 09 · 2026
Known for: 1 year
Remembered for: everything

For the one this sky remembers.

Some memories disappear with time. Some become part of the way we see everything after them.

This page is a small place for the things that deserved to stay.

A year is only a measurement. What it holds is much bigger: the ordinary days, the impossible ones, the laughter, the quiet, the moments that became memories before we even knew they would matter.

So I made a sky instead of a clock. Every light can disappear, but for a moment it still exists — bright enough to be seen, remembered, and kept.

Happy first anniversary.

Whatever the next chapters look like, this one will always have a place here.
